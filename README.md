# Atelier Croqui

**A fashion croquis studio for your computer.** Pick a model, dress it, try your own garment designs on it, change colours and fabrics, and export professional boards and tech sheets. It works offline and there is nothing to set up.

Available in **English and Portuguese**: switch with the **PT | EN** buttons at the top of the window.

## Quick start: try your own garment on a croquis

1. **Add a croquis** (*Croquis* tab): choose Woman, Man, Girl or Boy, a pose, front or back view and clothes. Click **Add croquis to the board**.
2. **Colour it:** click a garment, then pick a colour in the *Colours* tab.
3. **Add a fabric:** with a garment selected, open *Fabrics* and click a fabric or print.
4. **Bring in your own design:** press **U**, or drag an image into the window. PNG with a transparent background works best.
5. **Fit it to the body:** select your image, open *Settings → Warp (wrap)*. The croquis appears underneath. Click **Fit to body (automatic)**, drag the points to fine-tune, then **Apply warp**.
6. **Export** with **Ctrl+E**, or create a **Tech sheet**.

## What you can do

**Croquis**
- Woman, man, girl and boy, front and back, in clean fashion-figure line art
- Poses for the woman: standing, catwalk, contrapposto, hands on hips
- Poses for the man: standing, stride, relaxed, hands on hips, hand in pocket
- Skin tones (including a plain white "technical" figure), hair styles and colours
- Dresses, tops, shirts, coats, trousers, shorts, skirts, sneakers and boots, all recolourable

**Your designs and the wrap tool**
- Import PNG, JPG, WebP and SVG (menu, drag-and-drop or paste)
- Clip your design inside a garment, then move, rotate, resize and flip it
- **Mesh wrap** to fit your clothes to the croquis, with automatic body fit and presets (arch, wave, bulge, cylinder, perspective, skew, twist)
- Remove a white background, adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and hue, or tint the image

**Colours and fabrics**
- 21 built-in fabrics and prints (stripes, gingham, tartan, floral, denim, linen, leopard, tweed, satin…)
- Make your own fabric from a photo, or repeat your design as a pattern
- Scale, rotate and shift any pattern, and blend it with the garment colour
- Pastel palettes, ready-made collections and your own saved colours

**Drawing and layout**
- Pencil, fine liner, marker and soft brush, with a **symmetry** mode
- Lines, rectangles, ellipses, text, eyedropper and eraser
- Layers with opacity, 16 blend modes, drag-to-reorder, lock and hide
- Rulers, guides, grid, snapping, zoom and pan
- Unlimited undo and redo, and automatic recovery if the program closes unexpectedly

**Saving and exporting**
- Save your work as a `.fashion` project and reopen it any time (double-click the file)
- Export **PNG, JPG, PDF or SVG**, with an optional transparent background
- **Tech sheet PDF** with garment details, fabrics, trims and a size chart

## Tips
- Give your garment drawings a **transparent background**, or use *Remove white background* in the *Settings* tab.
- To change the board size (A4, A3, square…), click the layers icon at the top.
- To place several croquis side by side, choose a wide board such as *A3 landscape* or *Wide board*, then add croquis one after another.
- Hold **Shift** while dragging a wrap point to move its neighbours too.
- Your saved colours and your own fabrics are remembered between sessions.

## Questions

**Does it need internet?** No. Everything works offline.

**My project won't open.** Only `.fashion` files saved by Atelier Croqui can be opened. To bring in a picture, press **U** or drag it into the window.

**The program closed unexpectedly.** The next time you open it, it offers to restore your last session.

**The wrap tool doesn't fit the sleeves.** The automatic fit aligns the garment to the torso. Sleeves usually need a manual touch-up by dragging the mesh points.

## Current limitations
- No pen-pressure sensitivity yet
- Poses are front-facing (no three-quarter views), and the back view is simplified
- The wrap tool does not follow garment folds automatically
- Pantone colours are not included; use the built-in palettes and your own colour collections
